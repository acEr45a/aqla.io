Privacy Policy

Zero Data-Broker Policy:

AQLA Companion operates on a strict zero-data-broker policy. Telemetry is evaluated 100% on-device.

The extension listens locally for tab switch events and compares domain hostnames in volatile memory against a public distraction list (e.g., social media).
